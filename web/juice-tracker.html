Setup
1

Load the song catalog

Gets the list of songs, eras, credits, and release status from juicewrldapi.com.

Where song links go

By default each song opens in the juicewrldapi.com player. To send the links somewhere else, open a song there, copy the address from your browser, and paste it below. Every link updates to match, whether the address uses the site's long ID or its short one.

Loading fails? Save the catalog by hand
  1. Open the catalog page in your browser.
  2. Save it with ⌘S (or Ctrl+S) as a .json file.
  3. If the page shows a "next" link that isn't null, change page=1 to page=2 in the address bar and save that too. Repeat until "next" is null.
  4. Select all the saved files with “Use saved catalog files.”
2

Choose your music folder

Pick your Plex music folder, or drop it onto this box.

Your browser may ask to “upload” or view the folder. Nothing leaves your computer; the page only reads song info from the files.

Once both steps are done, your collection summary appears here.

Fix tags in your library

Writes song info from the catalog into your matched MP3, FLAC, and M4A files, so Plex shows consistent titles, artists, years, and eras. Review the changes below; only the files you tick are edited.

Options

Optional. Saves each original file there before its first edit. Pick a folder outside your Plex music folder.

    Duplicates in your library

    Songs that show up in your library more than once, including different versions matched through alternate titles, and files with the same title that aren't in the catalog. Nothing is deleted here. Compare the files, then remove any you don't want in Finder.

    File health

    Finds files with no tags, files whose title doesn't match the catalog, and (in the Mac app) files that may be damaged or cut off. Nothing changes until you click a fix.

    Library snapshot

    Every week the app saves a list of your files, tags and matched songs to iCloud Drive (or Documents if iCloud Drive is off), away from your music drive. If a drive fails, open a snapshot here to download what you had again.
